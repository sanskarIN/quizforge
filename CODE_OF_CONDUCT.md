# Code of Conduct

QuizForge is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- give actionable, technical feedback without personal attacks;
- welcome contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information;
- keep discussions relevant to the project;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, and repeated hostile behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate moderation actions when necessary to protect the community.

Reports should be made privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Include enough context for the maintainers to understand the incident, but do not publicly repost sensitive personal information.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project-managed community spaces, and situations where someone is officially representing QuizForge.
